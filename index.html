How the school runs on Nabeng SBA

Simple flow for the whole term

A clear path from setup to results — so every role knows what to do.

1

Set up the school

Admin creates the academic year, terms, classes and subjects, then assigns teachers.

2

Admit & organise students

Students are registered into classes. Portal accounts can be generated for student access.

3

Teach & assess

Teachers take attendance, set homework, follow the timetable and enter continuous assessment and exam marks.

4

Report & improve

Reports are generated, dashboards show performance, and the school prepares for the next term or year.
