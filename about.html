About Nabeng SBA

A modern school management and assessment platform designed to make academic administration easier, faster and more organized.

About The System

Simple Technology For Better School Management

Nabeng SBA is a digital school management system designed to help schools manage students, academic assessments, attendance, results, reports and communication from one convenient platform.

The system reduces the dependence on paper-based record keeping and makes it easier for teachers and school administrators to manage academic information.

Students can also access their academic information through the student portal, making the school experience more transparent and convenient.

What We Provide

Built For Modern Schools

Our Story

The Beginning Of Nabeng SBA

Nabeng SBA started from a simple idea: to make school administration easier through technology.

The system was developed to address some of the challenges schools face when managing student records, assessments, attendance and academic reports manually.

What began as a technology project has grown into a complete school management platform designed around the everyday needs of teachers, administrators and students.

The goal is not simply to replace paper with computers, but to create a smarter and more efficient way of managing school information.

The Developer

Behind Nabeng SBA

Nathaniel Asare Boabeng

Nabeng SBA was developed by Nathaniel Asare Boabeng, a technology enthusiast and educator with an interest in using software to solve practical problems in education.

His interest in technology and education led to the development of Nabeng SBA with the aim of providing schools with a simple digital platform for managing academic activities.

The development journey focuses on continuous improvement, listening to the needs of schools and creating tools that are practical for teachers, administrators and students.
